## Additional principles - continued

New additional principles go here, at the end of this file. The rationale for each working rule and the earlier additional principles are in [[Working Principles]].

**A delegated agent's verdict is a claim until the lead reads the line.** A verdict from a delegated agent, of any model tier - "not found", "not planted", "paused", "done", "will be flagged" - is a claim until the lead reads the live line it rests on. Kin to "A completion is a disk fact, not a claim" (your own claims); this one covers a delegate's claims. Agents err in both directions, most often on absence. Re-check every negative verdict and every verdict you will act on; sample only the rest. The mechanics live in their own homes: search-agent briefing (Cowork Build Environment 8.2), the review verifier step (meta-learning-review Step 2), tracking claims (session-closer Step 3.5), triage close verdicts (Review Automation).
